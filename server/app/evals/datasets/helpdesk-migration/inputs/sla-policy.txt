IT Helpdesk - Service Level Policy

Support hours
The service desk operates from 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday,
excluding public holidays. All response and resolution targets in this
policy are measured in business hours, so a ticket raised at 17:30 on
Friday has its clock start again on Monday morning.

Priorities
Priority one means a whole team is blocked and cannot work. A priority
one ticket pages the on-call engineer immediately, regardless of the
time of day, and the service desk manager is notified at the same time.

Priority two is a single person blocked with no workaround.

Priority three is a degraded experience with a workaround available.

Priority four is a request for information, or a change with no urgency.

Who sets priority
The requester sets the initial priority when raising the ticket. The
first responder confirms or corrects it, and must record a reason when
changing it.

Targets
First response within 4 business hours for priorities two, three and
four. Priority one is acknowledged within 15 minutes. Resolution targets
are 8 business hours for priority two and 3 business days for priority
three; priority four has no resolution target and is handled in the
weekly change window.
