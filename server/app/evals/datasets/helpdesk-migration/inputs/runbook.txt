Helpdesk Migration - Operations runbook (draft)

Ticket lifecycle
New, assigned, in progress, waiting on requester, resolved, closed.
A ticket closed without requester confirmation after 5 business days is
closed automatically, and the requester is told so.

Assignment
Tickets are assigned by category to a rota. The on-call engineer is the
escalation point outside business hours.

Priority
Priority is set by the requester and confirmed by the first responder.
Priority one means a whole team is blocked, priority four is a request
for information. Priority one tickets page the on-call engineer
immediately.

Channels
Email to support@ is converted into a ticket automatically. Replies by
email append to the ticket. The web portal is the only place to change
status.

Knowledge base
Articles are written by the resolver and reviewed quarterly. An article
can be marked obsolete but not deleted, so historical links keep
working.

Access
Access is granted through the Google Workspace directory group
helpdesk-users. Removing someone from the group removes portal access
within 15 minutes.

Data retention
Resolved tickets are retained for 3 years, then deleted. Backups are
kept for 30 days.

Reporting
The manager dashboard refreshes hourly, not in real time. A nightly CSV
export is available for the monthly service review.

Known constraint
The old shared mailboxes must stay readable for 90 days after the cutover
in case someone needs an old thread.
