IT Helpdesk - Migration and Cutover Plan

Training
Every staff member attends one training session before cutover. Sessions
run three times a week for a month, take 45 minutes, and cover raising a
ticket, replying by email and finding a knowledge base article. Team
leads get a second, longer session on the manager dashboard.

Parallel running
The old shared mailboxes and the new system run in parallel for two
weeks after cutover. During that window the desk triages both, so nobody
loses a request while people get used to the portal.

Old mailboxes
After the parallel period the shared mailboxes are closed to new mail
but stay readable for 90 days, in case someone needs an old thread. They
are archived after that.

Rollback
We roll back if more than 20% of tickets in a week are misrouted, or if
the desk cannot meet the first response target for three consecutive
days. Rollback means re-opening the shared inboxes as the primary
channel; the new system stays available read-only.

Comms
A single announcement goes out two weeks before cutover, a reminder the
day before, and a note in the weekly all-hands. There is no separate
launch event.

Cutover date
Planned for the first Monday after the training month completes. The
exact date is confirmed by the IT operations manager once the knowledge
base reaches twenty published articles.
