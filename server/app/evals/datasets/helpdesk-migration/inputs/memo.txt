IT Helpdesk Migration - Memo

To: All staff
From: Head of IT Operations
Subject: Moving off shared mailboxes

Why
Support requests arrive in four shared inboxes and a WhatsApp group.
Nothing is tracked, nobody owns a ticket, and we cannot report on how
long anything takes. We are moving to a ticketing system in the next
quarter.

What changes for you
Tickets are raised through a web portal, by email to a single support
address, or from the intranet. Every request gets a reference number.

There will be one queue with categories: hardware, software, access,
network and other. Staff can see the status of their own tickets; they
cannot see anyone else's.

Service targets
First response within 4 business hours. If a ticket is not resolved
within 8 business hours it escalates automatically to the on-call
engineer.

Anything marked as a security incident goes straight to the on-call
engineer and is not held in the queue.

Sign-in
Staff sign in to the portal with their existing Google Workspace
account. Single sign-on only; there will be no separate helpdesk
password.

Self-service
We will publish a knowledge base of the twenty most common fixes so
staff can solve simple problems without raising a ticket. The knowledge
base is searchable and public to all staff.

History
The last 12 months of email from the shared inboxes will be imported so
we do not lose the audit trail. Anything older stays in the archive and
is not migrated.

Reporting
Managers get a dashboard with ticket volume, first response time and
resolution time, filterable by team and category.

Not in scope
No mobile app. The portal is responsive and works in a phone browser,
and that is the end of it. Asset tracking is a separate project.
