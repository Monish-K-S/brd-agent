IT Helpdesk - Interview with the IT Operations Manager

Q: What is the real problem with the shared inboxes?
A: Nobody owns anything. A request arrives, four people read it, nobody
answers, and the requester asks again a week later. We cannot even say
how many tickets we handled last quarter, because counting emails is not
counting work.

Q: What does success look like a year from now?
A: Every request has an owner and a reference number. I can show the
finance director a number for first response time that is not a guess.
And the helpdesk stops being a place where people burn out.

Q: What worries you about the migration?
A: People hating it and going back to email. That is why training
matters more than the software. If someone cannot raise a ticket in
under a minute, they will go back to email.

Q: How much process is too much?
A: We are a team of six supporting two hundred and fifty people. Do not
build us a system for a thousand agents. Four priorities is already one
more than I wanted.

Q: Anything about the knowledge base?
A: I would rather have twenty good articles than a hundred that are out
of date. If it fills with stale content, staff stop trusting it and we
have wasted the effort.

Q: Reporting expectations?
A: Monthly, by team and by category. I need to see volume, first
response and resolution time. I do not need a live dashboard on the wall.

Q: What about people who leave?
A: Access has to come off the day they leave, not when someone
remembers. That is a security requirement, not a convenience.
