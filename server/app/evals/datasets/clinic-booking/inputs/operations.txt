Clinic Booking - Front Desk Operations Notes

Appointment length
Standard appointments are 15 minutes. Paediatric appointments are 20
minutes and are set that way by default in the doctor's profile. The
front desk can override the length when booking on a patient's behalf.

Confirming a booking
As soon as a booking is confirmed, the patient receives a confirmation
SMS with the clinic address, the doctor's name and the appointment time.
This is separate from the reminder that goes out 24 hours before.

Rescheduling
Patients can reschedule up to 2 hours before the appointment, on the
same terms as cancelling. After that, the desk handles it as a missed
appointment.

What the desk sees
Staff can look up a patient's booking history for the last 12 months,
including no-shows, because the doctor asks about it in the room.

No-show performance
The clinic director tracks no-shows monthly. The target is a no-show
rate of 10% or lower per doctor. Today the average is 18%, and the
director expects reminders and the waitlist to bring it down.

Phone line
The telephone booking line stays open for patients who cannot use the
app. Desk staff enter phone bookings into the same system, so every
appointment sits in one schedule.

Hardware
Each clinic desk has two terminals and a printer for the day sheet. The
system must print the day's appointments in time order at the start of
each shift.
