Clinic Appointment Booking - Requirements Document

Purpose
Replace the phone-only appointment process at the three city clinics
with an online booking service for patients.

Booking
Patients must be able to book an appointment online through the clinic
website and the patient mobile app.

Patients choose a doctor by specialty — general medicine, paediatrics,
dermatology or orthopaedics — and then pick from that doctor's available
slots.

When a patient selects a slot, the slot is held for 10 minutes while they
confirm. If they do not confirm within 10 minutes, the slot is released
back to the pool automatically.

The system must prevent double booking. Two patients must never be able
to hold the same slot at the same time.

If a doctor has no free slots, the patient can join a waitlist for that
doctor and is notified when a slot frees up.

Reminders
A reminder SMS is sent 24 hours before the appointment. Patients must be
able to cancel up to 2 hours before the appointment without a penalty.

Doctor availability
Doctors set their weekly availability themselves from a staff
dashboard, including leave days. Changes take effect immediately.

Payments
Phase one does not take online payments. Patients pay at the clinic
desk, in cash or by card.

Access
Staff need an admin dashboard showing the day's appointments per doctor
and per clinic, and the ability to reschedule on a patient's behalf.

Walk-in patients are still accepted at the desk and must appear on the
same schedule as booked patients so doctors see one list.
