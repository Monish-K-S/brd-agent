Clinic Booking - Stakeholder interview, Dr Nair (clinic director)

Transcript excerpt, lightly edited for length.

Q: What breaks most often today?
A: Double bookings. The front desk writes in a book, and two people call
for the same slot. It happens maybe five times a week across the three
clinics.

Q: What would make staff actually use the new system?
A: It has to be faster than the book. One screen, today's list, and the
ability to move someone to another slot without hunting. If it takes
more than a few clicks they will go back to paper.

Q: Anything about languages?
A: Yes. A large share of our patients read Hindi better than English, so
the patient-facing screens need Hindi as well as English. Staff screens
can stay English.

Q: Reminders?
A: One SMS the day before is enough. Do not send three; people complain.

Q: What about patients without smartphones?
A: The phone line stays open. Do not remove it. Walk-ins stay too.

Q: Privacy obligations?
A: We handle medical records, so patient data must be protected in line
with our clinical data policy. Access is limited to staff at the clinic
where the appointment is booked.

Q: Reporting?
A: I want a monthly report of no-show rates per doctor and per clinic.
That is the number I am judged on.

Q: Anything you explicitly do not want?
A: No online payment in the first version. Billing at the desk is
working, and adding payments would delay the launch.
