Study Room Booking - Product Note

Background
The college library has fourteen group study rooms and no way to book
them. Students currently claim a room by writing their name on a
whiteboard on the door, which leads to double bookings and arguments.
The library wants a small booking tool built into the student portal.

What students need
Students must be able to book a study room for up to two hours at a
time. A booking requires a college email address; personal email
addresses are not accepted, because the library can only contact
students through the college directory.

Students should be able to filter the room list by equipment, so a group
that needs a projector can see only the rooms that have one. Four of the
fourteen rooms currently have projectors.

If a group cancels a booking, the slot must become available to other
students immediately, not at the end of the day.

Out of scope
The first version will not send reminder emails, and it will not support
booking on behalf of another student.

Open question
Should a student be limited to one active booking at a time? The library
has not decided yet.
