Checkout - Support themes from the last quarter

We tagged 4,112 support tickets. The five recurring themes are below.
These are observations from the current checkout, not requirements.

1. "I did not want to create an account"
Roughly a third of the tickets start with a shopper abandoning the form
because they were asked for a password. Several mention that they only
wanted to buy one item.

2. "The form asked me for the same address twice"
Complaints cluster around the billing address being separate from the
shipping address with no copy option.

3. "My card was declined and I had no idea why"
Shoppers see a generic failure message. They retry, get declined again,
and call us.

4. "I could not use my coupon"
Discount codes entered from the cart page were silently dropped when the
shopper reached payment.

5. "It was too slow on my phone"
Load times above four seconds on mobile were mentioned in 240 tickets.

Also worth noting: shoppers frequently ask whether they can pay with
UPI. UPI is not supported today, and it is not in the phase one scope
described by the product brief.
