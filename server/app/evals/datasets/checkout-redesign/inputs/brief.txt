Checkout Redesign - Product Brief

Problem
Our storefront loses 68% of carts at the payment step. Analytics show
the three biggest drop-off points are forced account creation, a
six-field shipping form, and the absence of any digital wallet.

Goals
The redesign must reduce cart abandonment from 68% to 50% within two
quarters of launch. Everything below serves that number.

Scope for phase one
We will support guest checkout so a shopper can buy without creating an
account. Returning customers can save a card and reuse it later.

We will add Apple Pay and Google Pay as express options at the top of
the checkout page. Card payments through our existing processor remain
the default.

The shipping address form will use address autocomplete, and the order
summary will show an estimated delivery date before the shopper pays.

Discount codes must be applyable during checkout, not only from the cart
page.

Non-functional requirements
The checkout page must load in under two seconds on a mid-range Android
phone over 4G, because that is where most of our traffic comes from.

We must remain PCI DSS compliant, which means card data never touches
our servers.

Fraud screening runs on every order above 25,000 rupees.

Out of scope
Phase one is India only. We will price and charge exclusively in Indian
rupees, and we will not support cross-border shipping or foreign
currencies.

Buy-now-pay-later financing is deferred to phase two.
