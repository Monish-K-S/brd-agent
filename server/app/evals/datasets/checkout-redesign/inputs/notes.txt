Checkout Redesign - Engineering sync notes

Attendees: Priya (product), Daniel (engineering), Aisha (design),
Rahul (payments), Meera (support)

Payment retry
Rahul raised that 11% of card attempts fail on the first try because of
bank timeouts. We agreed the checkout will retry a failed card payment
automatically up to three times before showing an error, and the shopper
will see a clear message if all three attempts fail.

Fraud
Rahul confirmed the fraud threshold stays at 25,000 rupees for phase
one. Orders above that go to manual review, which can delay dispatch by
up to a day, and support must be able to see that state.

Saved cards
Daniel asked whether saved cards are opt-in. We agreed saved cards are
opt-in and the shopper can delete a saved card from the account page at
any time.

Session behaviour
Aisha pointed out that a shopper who closes the tab should not lose the
cart. The cart must persist for 30 days on the same device.

Analytics
We need an abandoned checkout event so growth can measure the 68% to 50%
target. Daniel will own that.

Open question
Do we show delivery estimates for pin codes we cannot serve yet? Nobody
had an answer, so it stays open.

Decision
We are not building a native app checkout this year. Mobile web is the
only surface in phase one.
