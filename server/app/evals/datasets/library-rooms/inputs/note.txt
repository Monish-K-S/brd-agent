Study Room Booking - Product Note

Background
The college library has fourteen group study rooms with no booking
system. Students claim a room by writing their name on the whiteboard on
the door, which leads to double bookings, arguments at the desk, and
rooms sitting empty because someone claimed one and never turned up.
The library wants a small booking tool built into the student portal.

What students need
Students must be able to book a study room for up to two hours at a
time. A booking requires a college email address; personal email
addresses are not accepted, because the library can only contact
students through the college directory.

Students can book up to 30 days in advance and cannot hold more than two
active bookings at once.

Students should be able to filter the room list by equipment, so a group
that needs a projector can see only the rooms that have one. Four of the
fourteen rooms currently have projectors.

If a group cancels a booking, the slot must become available to other
students immediately, not at the end of the day.

Library staff need to be able to block a room for maintenance. A blocked
room disappears from the student view for the duration of the block, and
any booking already made in that window is cancelled with a notification
to the student.

Out of scope
The first version will not send reminder emails, and it will not support
booking on behalf of another student. Rooms cannot be booked for
non-library use, such as society meetings.

Open question
Should a student be limited to one active booking at a time instead of
two? The library has not decided yet.
